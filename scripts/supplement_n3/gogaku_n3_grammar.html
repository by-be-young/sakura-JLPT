Anup Sensei’s recommended review method

For every mistake, identify the grammar form, how it connects to the preceding word, and the nuance it adds. For sentence-composition questions, rebuild the entire sentence. For text grammar, reread the sentences before and after the blank.

間違えた問題は、正解だけでなく「なぜその答えになるのか」「ほかの選択肢はなぜ違うのか」まで確認してから、1〜2日後にもう一度挑戦してください。

JLPT N3 Grammar Mock Test FAQ

JLPT N3「文法」第11回と、効果的な復習方法についてよくある質問をまとめました。

Is JLPT N3 grammar Set 11 free? 無料ですか？

Yes. You can complete the entire timed CBT and review every answer for free. はい、無料で受験でき、送信後にすべての解答と解説を確認できます。

How many questions are included, and how long is the timer?

Set 11 contains 23 questions and uses a 30-minute practice timer. It covers grammar-form selection, sentence composition and text grammar.

What can I see after submitting my answers? 送信後に何を確認できますか？

You can see your score and review your answer beside the correct answer. Each item includes English and Nepali support, the reason the correct option works, why the other choices are wrong, and key vocabulary and grammar notes. 自分の答え、正解、正解の理由、ほかの選択肢が違う理由を確認できます。

Can I resume an unfinished test?

Yes, an unfinished attempt can be resumed in the same browser while time remains. Closing the page does not pause the original countdown.

Is this an official JLPT examination paper?

No. This is an independent Gogaku practice resource based on questions from the displayed N3 workbook. It is intended for preparation and does not issue an official JLPT score.

Where can I download the JLPT N3 workbook PDF? 問題集PDFはどこで入手できますか？

Download the workbook PDF through the Gogaku – Learn Japanese Online app. GogakuアプリからN3問題集PDFをダウンロードできます。

How should I use all 15 grammar sets?

Take one set under timed conditions, review every mistake, and retry it after one or two days. Continue to the next set only after understanding why each missed option was wrong.
